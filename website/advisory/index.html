The questions that decide success

What I help you answer.

Bringing the clinical reality of the NHS to the questions that decide whether a health-tech product succeeds.

  • Is this solving a sufficiently important problem?
  • Who will pay for it?
  • What evidence will be needed?
  • What are the regulatory and clinical risks?
  • How does it fit into real-world workflows?
  • And can it scale?

How it works

Start with a conversation.

Products vary too much for a one-size-fits-all package. Advising on an AI scribe is a very different job from advising on an EPR, so the scope is agreed with you first.

  1. 01

    Free initial conversation

    What you're building or assessing, where you are, and what you need to know. No obligation.

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

    We agree what I'll look at and what you'll get, so the cost is clear before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Review and advice

    Time with the product, its documentation and your team, with clear, candid advice on what will help or hinder clinical adoption and NHS sales.

  4. 04

    Ongoing support, if useful

    I can stay on as a clinical adviser as the product develops, or act as your Clinical Safety Officer when you need one.
