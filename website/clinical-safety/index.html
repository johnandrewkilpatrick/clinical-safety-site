How it works

From first conversation to sign-off.

Every project follows the same core process, scaled to the size and clinical risk of the product.

  1. 01

    Understand the product and its risks

    A detailed discussion of how the product works, who uses it, where it sits in the clinical workflow, and where things could go wrong for patients.

  2. 02

    Review your documentation

    Policies, regulatory position, quality assurance and any existing safety documentation, so the safety case reflects how the product is actually built, deployed and supported.

  3. 03

    Hazard workshop

    A structured session with your clinical, product and technical people to identify hazards, their causes and the controls that reduce the risk.

  4. 04

    DCB0129 or DCB0160 documentation

    The clinical safety documentation the standard requires, including the clinical risk management plan, hazard log and clinical safety case report, signed off by me as your CSO.

  5. 05

    Ongoing CSO cover

    Safety assessment of new features and changes, and regular review of the hazard log, so your safety case stays current as the product develops.
