What I offer

Need a named CSO, a clinical sense-check, or training?

Health-tech work usually starts at a specific moment: an NHS customer has asked for a clinical safety case, a deployment needs signing off, an investor wants to know whether the clinical claims stand up, or a team needs to get up to speed. Every engagement starts with a free conversation.

01

Named Clinical Safety Officer

For manufacturers (DCB0129) and NHS organisations (DCB0160)

I act as your named CSO: understanding how the product works and where the risks are, reviewing your documentation, running the hazard workshop, producing the DCB0129 or DCB0160 safety documentation, and staying on to assess new features. Typically one to two months from contract to safety sign-off.

About me

Clinician, strategist, and health-tech insider.

I'm a GP partner and clinical leader with a decade of experience at the intersection of medicine, digital health, and AI. I've advised NHS ICBs, GP federations, and health-tech companies ranging from early-stage startups to established enterprise products — on clinical safety, regulatory positioning, product strategy, and identifying viable clinical markets.

My work sits at a crossroads that few people occupy: I understand what clinicians actually need, what the NHS will and won't adopt, and how to translate that into product and commercial decisions. I've seen health-tech succeed and fail, and I know which variables matter.

I'm increasingly focused on executive clinical leadership — working with high-growth health-tech and AI companies that want a clinical voice at the strategic table, not just in the compliance column.
